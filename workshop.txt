title=Slots [B42]
description=A slot machine mod for Project Zomboid, updated for Build 42 from the latest published B41 version.
description=
description=How to Use:
description=- Carry Base.Money in your main inventory.
description=- Walk up to a green Dr. Oids arcade machine.
description=- Right click it and choose Play Slots.
description=- Pick your bet and press SPIN.
description=
description=What It Does:
description=- Five bet tiers: $5, $15, $50, $75 and $100.
description=- Keeps the published B41 payout tables, weighted results and tiered jackpots.
description=- Money, 7, diamond and skull reel symbols.
description=- Reels stop one at a time with sound cues.
description=- Clear win, loss and jackpot results, with separate payout and net amounts.
description=- The slot window counts winnings up after the payout appears.
description=- SOUND ON/OFF button for the slot effects.
description=
description=Build 42 Notes:
description=- Tested in single-player on 42.21.0.
description=- Multiplayer validation and money settlement run on the server. Live multiplayer testing is still needed.
description=- Uses vanilla Base.Money. No economy mod is required.
description=- PhunMart, Bikini Tools and the old B41 casino extensions are not integrated into this release.
description=- Do not enable the B41 Slots mod alongside this version.
description=- Inventory settles immediately; the delayed count-up is in the slot window.
description=
description=For Server Owners / Admins:
description=Admin spawn: /additem "YourUsername" Base.SlotMachine 1
description=Place it with the normal furniture placement controls.
description=

description=Please report bugs, odd behavior and mod conflicts with your game version and the steps that caused them.
